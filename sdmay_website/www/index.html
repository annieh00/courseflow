Senior Design Team SDMAY26-48 - Course Flow [Team 2]

Project Overview


CourseFlow is a web-based platform designed to streamline course management for students and their respective advisors. Typically students have to rely on static flowchart PDFs to plan their academic journey, which can be cumbersone and annoying to update. CourseFlow aims to solve this problem by providing an interactive and dynamic interface that allows students to get a live view of their progress towards degree implementation. CourseFlow will also allow advisors to directly view and recommend courses to their advisees, making the advising process more efficient and effective.
